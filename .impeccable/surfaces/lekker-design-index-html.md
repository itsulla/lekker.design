# Lekker Homepage Surface Brief

## Thesis
Different briefs deserve different systems. The homepage leads with verified artifacts instead of agency claims.

## First viewport
Keep the two-line proposition, a sub-20-word studio definition, two actions, and the two-project proof stage visible at 1440x1000. The project frames oppose one another across the orange `//` mark.

## Story order
1. Opposed proof pair: Asset Screener and Maison Masque.
2. Asymmetric field of three live builds.
3. Four-discipline ledger linking to filtered portfolio archive states.
4. Three-verb process: Frame, Make, Refine.
5. Four-image range preview with explicit Live and Concept study labels.
6. One vermilion contact panel.

## Identity and constraints
Use the existing cool paper, near-black ink, vermilion accent, Bricolage Grotesque, square media, and pill controls. Reuse portfolio.css as the shared shell and add homepage rules in home.css. Keep English and Traditional Chinese synchronized, including alt text and accessible labels. Do not add testimonials, client logos, metrics, awards, gradients, or unsupported delivery promises.

## Motion
Use one staggered clip reveal for the opposed hero frames and short one-time scroll reveals. Honor reduced motion. No autoplay, parallax, scroll hijacking, or looping effects.

## Maintenance
Homepage proof can be edited in static HTML. The full catalog remains canonical in portfolio.js. Discipline links use `?filter=<discipline>#archive`, and portfolio.js owns filter URL state.
